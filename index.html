04 / How I work

Not just the words.
The system
around them.

01

Define the behaviour.

Turn “write clearly” into decisions, conditions and examples that a person or an agent can follow.

02

Ask when it matters.

Clarify when ambiguity changes the outcome. Use the context the service already holds.

03

Keep the evidence close.

Make the route from an answer to its source visible. Say when that source is missing.

04

Match care to consequence.

The more consequential the action, the stronger the confirmation and human oversight it needs.
