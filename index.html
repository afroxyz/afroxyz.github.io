How I work

Four principles that run through every project

Rules over aspirations

"Write clearly" doesn't change an agent's behaviour. Specific decisions, conditions and examples do.

Ask only when it changes the answer

Clarify when ambiguity changes the outcome. Otherwise use the context the product already holds.

Evidence you can trace

Every answer should lead back to its source, and say when it can't.

The consequence sets the confirmation

The greater the consequence of an action, the stronger the confirmation required. Human judgement stays in the system.

About

Content design for conversational and AI-assisted services

I'm a content and conversational designer at the Royal Borough of Greenwich, where I build AI-assisted workflows for the digital team, including a Claude Code content operations agent and a Copilot Studio feedback analyser.

My wider work covers conversation design for Voiceflow chatbots, UX writing for fintech and nonprofit products, and content systems in Drupal and SharePoint.

I'm particularly interested in the point where an agent has to say no, say "I don't know", or ask a good question. That is where the design shows.
