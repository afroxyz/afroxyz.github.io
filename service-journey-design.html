Work with me / 01

Complex service & journey design

For teams whose applications, eligibility journeys and digital services have become difficult to understand or complete.

Discuss a project

The starting point

When this helps

  • People struggle to work out whether a service is right for them or what to do next.
  • Application questions, evidence requirements and validation create avoidable effort.
  • Service rules and operational processes are hard to follow across the whole journey.

I would start with the task people need to complete, review the existing journey and speak with the people using and operating the service. Mapping the rules alongside the user journey helps reveal where a content issue is also a process or interaction problem.

A defined piece of work

What you would receive

Service and content audit
A review of the journey, its content, service rules and known points of failure.
Journey and decision maps
User journeys, eligibility logic, evidence requirements and the handoffs between teams.
Redesigned flows and interaction content
A prototype of the agreed journey, with questions, guidance, validation and recovery states.
Implementation and measurement plan
Recommendations for delivery, agreed success measures and a prioritised backlog.

Designing for the consequences

Rules, review and collaboration

Accessibility, sensitive questions and evidence recovery would be considered throughout the design. I would work with service specialists to confirm eligibility rules and with assessors or operational staff to check whether the resulting information supports their work.

A focused review or one priority journey, with scope agreed around the service and the team’s delivery capacity.

Built for the team to use

Handover and measuring value

Your team would receive editable journey maps, agreed interaction content, documented rules and a prioritised delivery backlog. A walkthrough would help designers, developers and service staff understand the decisions and the checks needed before release.

Depending on the service, measures could include completion, missing evidence, repeat contact, validation errors and assessment effort. The plan would define the baseline and how each measure will be collected.

What would you like to improve?

Tell me about the journey, the people using it and where your team is getting stuck. We can discuss a useful starting point and the scope of the work.
