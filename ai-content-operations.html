Work with me / 02

AI content operations & governance

For content teams dealing with inconsistent standards, repeated manual reviews, outdated guidance and fragmented knowledge.

Discuss a project

The starting point

When this helps

  • Different people or tools apply the same content standards inconsistently.
  • Quality checks and routine reviews consume time that the team needs for design work.
  • The team wants to explore AI but needs clear sources, review controls and a way to evaluate the output.

I would trace a real request through the team’s workflow, inspect the standards and examples it depends on, and identify where automation could remove repeat work. The first prototype would focus on one task with an output the team can review.

A defined piece of work

What you would receive

Content operations assessment
A map of requests, checks, publishing steps and the points where work or decisions are repeated.
Standards and knowledge map
An agreed source for the rules, examples, ownership and updates an AI-assisted workflow would need.
A bounded workflow prototype
One suitable use case developed into a working prototype, with explicit inputs, outputs and human review.
Evaluation and governance plan
Test scenarios, quality criteria, failure handling, review responsibilities and a plan for recording results.

Designing for the consequences

Rules, review and collaboration

The design would define which information a system may use, which actions need approval and who owns the standards. Evaluation would check accuracy, rule compliance, unsupported assumptions and the quality of the handoff to a person.

An operations review and one workflow prototype. Wider rollout would be shaped by evaluation results, data requirements and your team’s technical capacity.

AI Content Design OS

Eight specialised agents read shared standards, with repeatable audit checks and approval before outbound notifications. The system is in use on a form migration, with operational impact measures still being collected.

Content Operations Agent

A prototype test on 12 historical tickets recorded median completion time of 18 minutes manually and 6 minutes with the agent. The study also documents failure cases used to improve the workflow.

Built for the team to use

Handover and measuring value

Your team would receive the prototype, its instructions and knowledge sources, evaluation scenarios and results, and a guide to maintaining the rules. A walkthrough would show how to review outputs, report failures and decide whether the workflow is ready for wider use.

Measures would be agreed for the selected task, such as review time, first-pass acceptance, corrections and unsupported claims. Repeat testing would show whether changes improve quality and reduce effort.

What would you like to improve?

Tell me about the journey, the people using it and where your team is getting stuck. We can discuss a useful starting point and the scope of the work.
